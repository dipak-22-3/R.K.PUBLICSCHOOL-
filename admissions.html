Admissions 2026-27

Join the RKPS family. Discover the steps to enroll your child for a bright future.

Step-by-Step Admission Process

1

Registration & Form Filling

Parents can collect the registration form from the school reception or fill out the initial enquiry form online. The form must be completely filled with accurate details.

2

Interactive Session / Test

Based on the class applied for, an informal interactive session (for pre-primary) or a basic written evaluation (for higher classes) will be scheduled to understand the child's academic level.

3

Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification. Please keep the following ready:

  • Birth Certificate of the child
  • Aadhaar Card (Parents & Child)
  • 4 Passport size photographs
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school

Fee Submission & Enrollment

Once approved by the Principal, parents can submit the admission fee at the accounts office to confirm the seat. A welcome kit and uniform details will be provided.
