Academic Programs

Building a strong, stress-free foundation for young minds from Nursery to Class IV.

Our Teaching Philosophy

At RK Public School, we believe that early childhood is the most critical phase for cognitive and emotional development. Following modern educational guidelines, our curriculum for Nursery to Class IV is designed to be highly interactive, completely free of rote-learning, and deeply focused on foundational literacy and numeracy.

Pre-Primary Wing

Our Pre-Primary curriculum is deeply rooted in the "Play-Way" and Montessori methodologies. We understand that little kids learn best when they are happy, active, and exploring their surroundings. Our vibrant classrooms and caring teachers ensure a home-away-from-home environment.

Key Focus Areas:

Cognitive Development Gross & Fine Motor Skills Sensory Learning Social Etiquettes

Core Learning Topics:

Phonics & Alphabets Basic Numeracy (Counting & Shapes) Rhymes & Storytelling Colors & Patterns

Daily Activities:

  • Art & Craft: Clay modeling, coloring, and paper crafts to boost creativity.
  • Circle Time: Interactive sessions for language development and confidence building.
  • Free Play: Supervised playtime in our safe indoor and outdoor activity areas.

Primary Wing

As students step into Class I to IV, we gently transition them into structured learning while maintaining a stress-free environment. Heavy school bags and rote memorization are strictly discouraged. We focus on conceptual clarity, spoken English, and interactive learning through Smart Classes.

Core Subjects:

English Language Hindi (2nd Language) Mathematics Environmental Studies (EVS)

Co-Curricular & Skill Subjects:

Computer Basics / IT General Knowledge (GK) Moral Science / Value Education Art, Music & Dance Physical Education (PE)

Evaluation & Assessment:

  • No Stress Exams: We follow Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) for a fear-free testing environment.
  • Interactive Tests: Assessments via projects, oral quizzes, and smart-board activities rather than just pen-and-paper.
  • Spoken English Focus: Special emphasis on conversational English inside the campus.

Learning Beyond Classrooms

Smart Classrooms

Activity Room

Kid's Library
