Academic Programs

Building a strong, stress-free foundation for young minds from Nursery to Class IV.

Our Teaching Philosophy

At RK Public School, we believe that early childhood is the most critical phase for cognitive and emotional development. Following modern educational guidelines, our curriculum for Nursery to Class IV is designed to be highly interactive, completely free of rote-learning, and deeply focused on foundational literacy and numeracy.

Pre-Primary Wing

Our Pre-Primary curriculum is deeply rooted in the "Play-Way" and Montessori methodologies. We understand that little kids learn best when they are happy, active, and exploring their surroundings.

Key Focus Areas:

Cognitive Development Sensory Learning Social Etiquettes
  • Art & Craft: Clay modeling, coloring, and paper crafts to boost creativity.
  • Circle Time: Interactive sessions for language development.

Primary Wing

As students step into Class I to IV, we gently transition them into structured learning while maintaining a stress-free environment. Heavy school bags and rote memorization are strictly discouraged.

Core Subjects:

English Language Hindi (2nd Language) Mathematics Environmental Studies (EVS)
  • No Stress Exams: We follow Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE).
  • Interactive Tests: Assessments via projects, oral quizzes, and smart-board activities.
