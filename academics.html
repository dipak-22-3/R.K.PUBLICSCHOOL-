Academic Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed for 21st-century learners.

Our Curriculum Structure

Select a wing below to explore subjects and teaching methodologies.

Pre-Primary Wing

Our early childhood program focuses on play-way methods, sensory motor skills, and foundational learning. We provide a nurturing environment where little ones learn through exploration and creative activities.

Key Focus Areas:

Alphabet & Phonics Basic Numeracy Art & Craft Rhymes & Storytelling Motor Skills

Primary Wing

The primary years focus on building a strong academic foundation while encouraging curiosity. The curriculum is interactive and integrates technology to make learning engaging.

Core Subjects:

English Hindi Mathematics Environmental Science (EVS) Computer Basics Value Education

Secondary Wing

Preparing students for higher academic challenges. The secondary curriculum strictly follows the board guidelines with a focus on practical experiments, analytical thinking, and career readiness.

Core Subjects:

English Language & Literature Mathematics (Standard/Basic) Science (Physics, Chemistry, Bio) Social Science (His, Geo, Civics) Information Technology
